# Reading the generated boards

The boards establish appearance, silhouettes, staging and effect language. The model JSON, written briefs and canonical animation contract control names, stats, timing, target limits and final asset dimensions. Small generated lettering is illustrative.

- The Bar board's collection title is “The Wayfarer Bar”; the story location is The Bent Lantern.
- Recruitment and interface boards show layout studies. The searchable model catalog gives exact current ability effects, two-ability slots and baseline stats. Other HUD numbers can illustrate a particular scene or remain composition placeholders.
- The storyboard includes background cast and epilogue portrait placeholders. Final story art must replace them with the selected canonical characters and the five general models; placeholder names do not add recruits to the 58-model roster.
- Tornel's motion study contains eight exploratory positions per row. Those are pose studies, not a slicing contract. Production walk/basic clips use six frames, run uses eight, and ability clips use eight with release at one-based frame four. Final production must retain an actor body on every frame and separate any thrown hatchet/VFX layer.
- Map paintings are composition studies. Exact route nodes, raised-crossing rules, tower centers and deployment constraints are in `data/world.json`; do not trace coordinates from the paintings.
- Portraits in the HTML catalog are display windows into unchanged reference boards. They are not separately exported 96×96 production portrait assets.
- Three image corrections were requested during review: Senna's ceramic tools replace an invented bow; the interface uses the four canonical classes and Tornel's card; Tornel's Ashen Tempo is a five-second attack-speed buff rather than an orbiting damage attack.

Generated contact sheets still need an artist's consistency pass before sprite production: equal pixel scale, exact indexed colors, hands, props, pose spacing, directional equipment, clean silhouettes and readable final typography. No claim of finished engine atlases or tested gameplay follows from these reference images.
